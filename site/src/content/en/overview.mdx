# AnyTTY documentation

AnyTTY runs tasks on your computer or server and lets you view and operate them from the TUI, mobile App or CLI.

## Step one: give the guide to your agent

Send the message below to your own agent and add your question. It can read `llm.txt` on GitHub, then explain operations, look up settings or help diagnose your setup.

<AgentGuideStart locale="en" />

Try “Help me install AnyTTY and create a terminal,” “How do I change TUI shortcuts?” or “Why can't my phone connect?” Continue asking in your agent without reading the entire documentation first.
