# Frequently asked questions

These answers clarify common behavior and link to detailed guides.

## Must I start the daemon every time?

No. `anytty` starts it when the TUI needs it. Manual commands serve maintenance and unattended setup.

## Do tasks survive leaving the TUI or App?

They continue while the target machine and daemon run. Shutdown and daemon restart are different; see <DocLink locale="en" path="concepts/lifecycle">lifecycle</DocLink>.

## Is Cloud required?

No. Local, SSH and Direct work independently. Cloud optionally provides cross-network discovery and connectivity.

## Why pair after Cloud sign-in?

Accounts/enrollment and client authorization are separate. The target daemon issues grants to specific clients.

## Are phone and desktop terminals independent?

Opening the same target means one shared process. Input and resizing can affect others. Create another terminal for independent work.

## Is a follower read-only?

No. The role coordinates size, not complete input/file permissions. See <DocLink locale="en" path="clients/tui/size">sizing</DocLink>.

## Why are my copy keys different?

Built-in, recommended and custom configurations differ. Check current help and <DocLink locale="en" path="clients/tui/config/shortcuts">binding configuration</DocLink>.

## Does TUI YAML configure the phone?

No. App fonts, themes and the petal menu use App settings. Workbench layouts are also separate from theme YAML.

## Why did a task fail after wait succeeded?

Wait success means the requested state was reached. Inspect the program’s `exit_code`.

## Can the App preview every format?

Native preview focuses on size-limited text and images. Download other formats for an appropriate application. See <DocLink locale="en" path="files/transfers">preview limits</DocLink>.

## Where are Web tutorials?

This edition covers TUI, App and CLI. Web remains in product diagrams; tutorials follow its interaction improvements.
