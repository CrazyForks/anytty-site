# P2P, Relay and limits

Cloud can coordinate P2P or use Relay forwarding. The resulting path depends on network conditions, policy and available service capabilities.

## Path meaning

P2P attempts direct client-to-target exchange. Relay forwards when a direct path is unavailable or not selected. Both retain daemon ownership and identity/authorization requirements.

## Configure policy

```sh
anytty endpoint policy set ENDPOINT --route cloud --cloud-path auto
```

For diagnosis, help also exposes `p2p`, `relay` and `smart_route`, with Relay transport `auto`, `udp` or `tcp`. Verify changes in a new session. Forced P2P may fail rather than fall back to Relay.

## Limits and accounting

Route features, smart selection, Relay transports and quotas may depend on the service plan and state. Inspect current service information when a capability or quota error appears; recreating terminals will not resolve it.

P2P and Relay may be accounted for differently. Current service and purchase information define prices and quotas; this guide makes no fixed latency guarantee.

## Diagnostic evidence

Record network, actual path, time, versions and redacted errors. Test individual paths before automatic selection. Relay forwards protected traffic but does not issue terminal/file grants. The service still processes operational metadata; see <DocLink locale="en" path="help/security">security and privacy</DocLink>.
