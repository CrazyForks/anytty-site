# Remote devices

The TUI displays configured remote devices as endpoints. Prepare connectivity and pairing first, then choose devices and open or create tasks in the workbench.

## Prepare an endpoint

Choose <DocLink locale="en" path="connections/overview">SSH, Direct or Cloud</DocLink>, then register the device through pairing or supported CLI configuration. The TUI Connections page primarily edits, enables/disables and refreshes existing endpoints; it is not a complete pairing wizard.

## Inspect Connections

Press `Ctrl+G → e`. Check the device label, enabled state, routes and connection status.

<ScreenshotSlot id="tui-connections" locale="en" />

Built-in actions are `Enter` edit, Space enable/disable and `r` refresh. After changing routes or selection policy, establish a new connection to evaluate the new configuration.

A saved endpoint is not necessarily reachable; a connected device does not prove a task succeeded.

## Open remote terminals

Press `Ctrl+F`, switch device with left/right, then open an existing task or select New Terminal. Verify `server` and `workdir`: remote directories can differ from local ones.

Use descriptive task names and consider including the endpoint in pane titles with `tui.chrome.pane_title_template`. See <DocLink locale="en" path="clients/tui/config/appearance">appearance</DocLink>.

## Diagnose offline devices

Check that the target machine and daemon are running, then examine addressing, network and authorization. Use `anytty endpoint test ENDPOINT` for protocol reachability; see <DocLink locale="en" path="clients/cli/connections">CLI device commands</DocLink>. Disabling or removing a local endpoint does not revoke its target-side grant.
